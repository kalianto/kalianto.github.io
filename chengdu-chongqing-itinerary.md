## Ticket Booking Summary — Where to Book

- **12306** (official China Railway app/site) — for all high-speed rail tickets; supports passport for foreigners, has English app interface. Book as early as booking window opens (usually 15 days ahead).

- **Official WeChat mini-programs** (search the scenic area's Chinese name in WeChat) — often the primary/cheapest source and sometimes the only way to get the real-name timed-entry slots; Chinese phone number may be needed.
